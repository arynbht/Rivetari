At review-at-pr and unattended, the judge never passes a round with an unverified acceptance criterion. Tier 2 now reports each criterion as met, unmet or unverified, and is told to say unverified rather than guess met when the diff, the tests and the proof don't show it; a criterion it doesn't report on counts as unverified too. Such a round stops with an `unverified_acceptance` question that names each criterion and why, so `rivetari task run` exits 2; `--allow` ships it with the answer recorded, `--deny` sends it back to build with the note. At supervised the round passes and the ledger records the unverified criteria for the person reviewing the PR. The review now sees the task's proof (the proof lines, the test run, and per screenshot what the proof runner measured: page width against the viewport, elements past its edge, the start of the page's text). On a fix card with a UI proof the reviewer must say whether the before shows the defect; if it doesn't, the criteria that depend on it are unverified. Changed Markdown brings the repo files its added lines cite in backticks, at head: only files that exist, never sensitive, generated, binary or secret-shaped ones, at most 20,000 characters each and 60,000 in all.
